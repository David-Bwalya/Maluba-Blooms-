HOW TO CONNECT INSTAGRAM (one-time setup, about 10 minutes, free)
==================================================================

Once connected, the website shows EVERY post from @maluba_blooms (old and
new) and picks up new posts by itself within 30 minutes of you posting.
Until then, the site shows the backup list of posts in config.php.

STEP 1 - Make the Instagram account a Professional account
  In the Instagram app: Settings > Account type and tools >
  Switch to professional account > choose "Business" or "Creator".
  (Free. Nothing changes for your followers.)

STEP 2 - Create a Meta developer app
  1. Go to https://developers.facebook.com and log in with Facebook.
     Click "Get Started" if it's your first time.
  2. Click "My Apps" > "Create App".
  3. Give it a name, e.g. "Maluba Blooms Website".
  4. When asked for a use case, choose:
       "Manage messaging & content on Instagram"
  5. Finish creating the app.

STEP 3 - Generate the access token
  1. In the app dashboard, open:
       Instagram > "API setup with Instagram login"
  2. Under "Generate access tokens", click "Add account" and log in
     as maluba_blooms. Allow the permissions it asks for.
  3. Click "Generate token" next to the account, then copy the long token.

STEP 4 - Paste it into the website
  Open instagram/config.php and paste the token between the quotes:

      'access_token' => 'PASTE-THE-TOKEN-HERE',

  Save the file and refresh the website. Done.

NOTES
  - The token lasts 60 days, but the website renews it by itself every week.
    It only expires if nobody visits the site for about 2 months. If that
    happens, repeat Step 3 and Step 4.
  - The website must be hosted somewhere that runs PHP (most hosting does;
    XAMPP does on this computer).
  - Keep the token private. The instagram/ folder is locked so website
    visitors can't open it.
